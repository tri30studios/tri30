01. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how TRi30 Studios ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, processes, and protects information in connection with games and applications published under the TRi30 Studios name (collectively, the "Games") and our official website, www.tri30studios.com (the "Website").

The policy covers the TRi30 Studios published games to which it is linked, including titles such as Snake Rush Endless, Ghost Run, Jugaad – Fix Anything, and Breaker Evolution when published by TRi30 Studios. It also applies to future TRi30 Studios titles that link to this policy. It does not automatically cover games published by other companies, even if we provide development or support services for them.

We respect your privacy and aim to process only the information reasonably needed to operate, improve, secure, and provide features of our Games and Website.

Important: Individual Games do not necessarily have the same features or services. Leaderboards, accounts, analytics, advertising, purchases, cloud saves, and other functionality apply only where they are actually present in the relevant published version. App-store privacy disclosures, in-game notices, or additional game-specific notices may provide more detail when required.

02. Information We May Collect

Depending on the Game, its platform and version, enabled features, and how you interact with us, we may process the following categories:

  • Gameplay and progress information: Scores, game statistics, levels, achievements, missions, rewards, virtual items, selected skins, in-game settings, and related progress.
  • Player identifiers and accounts: Randomly generated, anonymous-authentication, or pseudonymous account identifiers, where required to maintain features such as saves, multiplayer, or leaderboards. Some Games may offer optional sign-in. An anonymous authentication ID is not necessarily anonymous data under every privacy law.
  • Leaderboard and social game information: Display names or generated nicknames, submitted scores, rankings, timestamps, and multiplayer or competition-related records, where those features exist. Display names and rankings may be visible to other players.
  • Device, diagnostics, and usage information: Depending on included software development kits (SDKs) and settings, device or operating-system details, app version, crash logs, performance diagnostics, network information, approximate location inferred from IP address, and interaction or event information.
  • Advertising and purchase information: Where applicable, advertising identifiers, ad interaction data, consent preferences, and transaction status or purchase receipts needed to fulfill purchases. Payments made through an app store or payment provider are generally handled by that provider; we do not need your full payment-card details to deliver ordinary game features.
  • Support, review, and community information: Information you choose to share when contacting us, leaving an app-store review, or interacting with an official social community, such as a public display name, message, email address, and issue details. Public posts are also subject to the relevant platform's privacy terms.
  • Website information: Information you send to us by email, plus limited technical information that may be processed by our website host, such as IP addresses, browser details, requested pages, access times, and security logs. Our current static website does not intentionally install third-party analytics or advertising trackers.

Not every Game collects every category above. Specific data collection depends on the actual release build and associated providers.

03. How We Use Information

  • Provide, operate, maintain, and improve our Games and Website.
  • Save or synchronize progress, scores, rewards, settings, and account information where supported.
  • Enable leaderboards, multiplayer, events, community features, and other player-facing functionality where offered.
  • Validate scores, detect cheating, prevent fraud and abuse, and maintain the security of our services.
  • Analyze stability, diagnose crashes, measure game performance, and improve player experience.
  • Deliver and measure advertising or fulfill in-app purchases when relevant to a particular Game.
  • Respond to support requests, app-store reviews, player feedback, and business inquiries.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations and enforce relevant policies.

04. Third-Party Services

Some Games may use services such as Google Firebase, Supabase, platform game services, hosting providers, analytics tools, crash-reporting tools, authentication services, or security providers. They may process data to provide the specific functionality included in a Game.

Other Games may use different providers or no cloud services. We do not represent that Firebase, Supabase, or any particular SDK is used in every title. Where required, applicable app-store disclosures and in-game notices will provide additional information about the services in the released version. Each third party's processing is also subject to its own privacy terms and applicable law.

Our Website may use web hosting and email providers to deliver pages and receive messages. The Website files in this package do not include a third-party analytics script, advertising pixel, or newsletter tracking service; this may change if features are added, in which case the notice should be updated.

05. Advertising and In-App Purchases

Certain Games may display advertisements or offer optional purchases. Where advertising SDKs are present, providers may process device, advertising, diagnostic, interaction, or consent data to deliver, limit, measure, or, where permitted, personalize advertisements. You may have platform or privacy controls for ad personalization.

Where a Game offers purchases, app stores or payment providers may process the payment and provide us with limited transaction information necessary to grant items, restore purchases, prevent fraud, and resolve support issues. If a published Game has no advertisements or purchases, the corresponding provisions do not apply to that Game.

06. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share or permit processing of relevant information by service providers that help deliver Games or Website functions, including hosting, authentication, analytics, support, security, crash reporting, app-store payments, and advertising services, where those services are actually used.

When a feature such as a leaderboard or multiplayer profile is enabled, certain submitted information such as a player nickname, score, or ranking may be visible to other players. We may disclose information where required by law or legal process, or where reasonably necessary to protect users, our services, or others from fraud and harm.

07. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to reduce risks of unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or loss. No internet transmission, app, or electronic storage system is completely secure.

08. Data Retention

We retain information only as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described in this policy, including gameplay and account functionality, progress synchronization, security, fraud prevention, customer support, dispute resolution, and legal obligations.

Depending on the relevant Game, some leaderboard or gameplay records may be archived, reset, rotated, deleted, or anonymized as part of feature operations. Retention periods may differ by Game, provider, and type of record. Uninstalling a Game does not necessarily erase information stored on our servers or by third-party services.

09. Data Deletion Requests

You may request deletion of information associated with a TRi30 Studios Game or the Website by emailing hello@tri30studios.com. Where an in-game or platform account-deletion option is provided, you may also use that option.

To help us locate the correct information, please tell us the name of the Game, your platform (Android, iOS, or web), and any player nickname or account identifier visible to you, if available. For Website requests, describe the correspondence or information involved. Do not email passwords, full payment-card numbers, or other sensitive credentials.

We may need to verify that you are authorized to request deletion. After a valid request, we will delete or anonymize relevant information as required by applicable law, subject to records we must or may retain for legal, security, or fraud-prevention reasons. Some data independently controlled by an app store or third-party platform may need to be managed through that provider.

10. Children's Privacy

Our Games may have different age ratings and audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. We do not request names, email addresses, phone numbers, or similar direct identifiers from children as a condition of ordinary gameplay unless a particular feature lawfully requires information and appropriate safeguards apply.

For Games that are directed to children or may be used by children, we will assess applicable age-related requirements, including restrictions on advertising, tracking, data collection, and parental consent where relevant. If you believe a child has provided us with information improperly, contact us using the details below so we can investigate.

11. International Data Processing

Depending on the hosting, cloud, advertising, and other services used by a Game or our Website, information may be processed in countries other than your country of residence. We take reasonable steps to address applicable requirements for such processing and transfers.

12. Your Choices and Rights

You may stop using a Game or our Website at any time. Where available, you can adjust game settings, permissions, advertising preferences, notifications, and privacy controls through the Game, operating system, or relevant app store.

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. Contact us to exercise these rights. We may ask for reasonable verification and will handle valid requests as required by law. If we introduce optional tracking technologies on the Website, we will update relevant notices and consent choices as appropriate.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy when our Games, Website, service providers, features, or legal obligations change. We will publish the revised version at https://tri30studios.github.io/tri30/privacy.html and update the "Last updated" date. Where required by law, we will provide additional notice.
