Ghost Run

Your toughest opponent is the run you just finished.

A time-limited challenge with a twist: past attempts return as ghosts. What begins as a solo race becomes an evolving test of timing, movement, and learning from yourself.

✳ THE IDEA

Beat yourself. Then do it again.

Ghost Run explores a simple, memorable idea: every attempt leaves behind a new runner. The accumulating ghost paths turn repetition into fresh pressure, making improvement visible and turning the player’s own history into gameplay.
