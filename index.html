✳ GOOD TO KNOW / 05

A few things
worth asking.

Don't see your question? Send us a note and we'll point you in the right direction.

Can you support an idea from concept to release? +

Our services cover game development, art, submission support, and player-facing operations. The exact scope depends on the project, its stage, and the capabilities needed.

Do you also work on existing games? +

Yes. You can reach out about a focused feature, visual refresh, release preparation, player support, or an ongoing engagement rather than an entirely new game.

Can you guarantee a game will be approved by app stores? +

No studio can guarantee platform approval. Our submission support focuses on preparation, identifying potential issues, and helping teams navigate the review process.

What does your player experience support include? +

We help manage Google Play and App Store reviews, prepare professional player responses, support Discord, Facebook, and X communities, and deliver weekly reports covering player sentiment, recurring issues, and practical recommendations based on reviews.

Where can I play your games? +

Our game pages introduce each project. Verified store links, platforms, and release information will appear there once available.

How do we start a conversation? +

Email hello@tri30studios.com with a short overview, what you're trying to achieve, and the type of support you need.
